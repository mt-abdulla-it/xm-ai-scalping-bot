# Forex and MT4 Basics

This skill provides reference knowledge for fundamental Forex trading mechanics and the MT4 platform, based on the Tradepedia Introduction to Forex course.

## 1. Currency Pairs
*   **Base vs. Quote**: In `EUR/USD`, EUR is the Base Currency, USD is the Quote Currency. `EUR/USD = 1.3500` means 1 EUR = 1.3500 USD.
*   **Majors**: The 7 most traded pairs involving the US Dollar (Very liquid, less volatile, tighter spreads). e.g., EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, USD/CHF, USD/CAD, AUD/USD, NZD/USD.
*   **Crosses**: Pairs not involving the US Dollar (Less liquid, more volatile, wider spreads). e.g., EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY, EUR/GBP.
*   **Exotics**: Pairs involving an emerging economy currency (Not liquid, very volatile, huge spreads). e.g., USD/TRY, USD/MXN.

## 2. Forex Mechanics
*   **Pip (Point in Price)**: The smallest price change a given exchange rate can make.
    *   Usually the **4th decimal point** (e.g., 1.305**8**). Some platforms use 5 decimals (e.g., 1.3058**3**).
    *   For **Japanese Yen (JPY)** pairs, it is the **2nd decimal point** (e.g., 110.4**5**).
*   **Spread**: The difference between the **Ask** (buy price) and **Bid** (sell price).
*   **Lots**: Standardized trading sizes.
    *   **Standard Lot** = 100,000 units of the base currency.
    *   **Mini Lot** = 10,000 units.
    *   **Micro Lot** = 1,000 units.
*   **Leverage & Margin**: Leverage is a loan provided by the broker (e.g., 100:1). Margin is the collateral required to open the leveraged position.
*   **Long vs. Short**:
    *   **Long**: Buy first, then Sell (Profit when price goes UP).
    *   **Short**: Sell first, then Buy (Profit when price goes DOWN).

## 3. MT4 Platform Basics
*   **Market Watch**: Window containing the symbol list and Bid/Ask quotes.
*   **Navigator**: Groups Accounts, Expert Advisors (EAs), Indicators, and Scripts.
*   **Terminal**: Bottom window for monitoring active trades, account history, news, and logs.
*   **Orders**:
    *   **Market Order**: Executes immediately at current Ask/Bid price.
    *   **Pending Orders**: Buy Limit, Sell Limit, Buy Stop, Sell Stop.
    *   **Stop Loss (SL)**: Minimizes losses if price moves against the trade.
    *   **Take Profit (TP)**: Locks in gains when price reaches a certain level.
