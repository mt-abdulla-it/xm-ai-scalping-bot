# Qualtrics XM Masters Program

When the user asks questions or requests tasks related to the Qualtrics Experience Management (XM) Masters program, use the following knowledge base:

## Program Overview
- **Duration**: 12 weeks.
- **Format**: 6 learning modules combining in-person and online learning (averaging 6-8 hours/week).
- **Tracks**: Students must choose either the **Customer Experience (CX)** or **Employee Experience (EX)** track for modules 3-5.
- **Provider**: Qualtrics XM Institute (a leading experience management think tank).

## Enrollment Qualifications
- Best suited for mid-to-senior level professionals.
- **Prerequisite 1**: 5+ years of XM experience (practitioner or consultant) in customer, employee, brand, or product experience.
- **Prerequisite 2**: Access to a current or past XM program to apply practical skills during the coursework.

## Attendance & Certification Requirements
- **Attendance**: Minimum 80% attendance required in all mandatory coursework.
- **In-person Workshops**: 4 days total (2 days in Week 1, 2 days in Week 12). Must be attended in person (locations: Dublin, Seattle, Sydney for 2023).
- **Final Project**: Satisfactory completion of the Professional Development Plan in Week 12.
- *Note*: It is not a graded certification.

## Code of Conduct & IP
- **Academic Integrity**: Plagiarism and cheating are strictly prohibited.
- **Personal Certification**: The XM Masters certification (XMM) and logo can *only* be used by the individual person who received it. A company, school, or organization cannot represent itself as certified.
- **Material Attribution**: XM Institute consulting toolkits and materials can be used but must attribute Qualtrics XM Institute as the author and cannot be publicly reproduced without permission.
