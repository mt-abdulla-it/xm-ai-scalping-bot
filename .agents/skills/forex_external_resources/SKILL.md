# External Forex & Trading Resources

Use this knowledge base when suggesting third-party educational content, trading strategies, or ways to reduce trading costs via rebates.

## Educational Platforms
*   **Trade That Swing (tradethatswing.com)**: 
    *   **Focus**: No-BS day trading, swing trading, and investing strategies for both Stocks and Forex.
    *   **Offerings**: Premium trading courses (e.g., Overcoming FOMO Masterclass, EURUSD Day Trading, Stock Swing Trading), in-depth articles on ETFs, and historical market statistics.
    *   **Recommendation**: Ideal for traders looking for structured, methodology-based swing and day trading courses independent of broker-sponsored material.

## Rebates & Cashback Services
*   **HighFxRebates (highfxrebates.com)**:
    *   **Focus**: Forex and Crypto Exchange cashback and rebate provider (operating since 2008).
    *   **Benefits**: Traders can earn up to 90% cashback on their trading volumes regardless of whether trades are won or lost. 
    *   **Features**: Provides broker comparisons, account type comparisons, and detailed lists of forex rebate rates across multiple brokers.
    *   **Recommendation**: Highly recommended for high-volume traders (like scalpers) to recover spread/commission costs and increase overall profitability.
